## How to Actually Find American Creators

The biggest problem with searching OnlyFans directly is that the platform itself **does not support location filtering**. You can't tell OnlyFans to show you only US creators, or only Miami creators, or only verified Texans. That's exactly why directory sites exist.

Here's the most effective workflow we've found, in order:

### 1. Start with a state or city page

If you have a specific location in mind, jump straight to the state page (e.g. [/california-onlyfans/](/california-onlyfans/), [/texas-onlyfans/](/texas-onlyfans/)) or the city page ([/los-angeles-onlyfans/](/los-angeles-onlyfans/), [/miami-onlyfans/](/miami-onlyfans/)). These are pre-filtered to verified American creators in that geography only.

### 2. Layer category filters

On any state or city page you can combine the location filter with categories like MILF, Latina, fitness, etc. by using the category dropdown. The result is a tightly filtered list — for example "verified Florida MILF creators under $10/month".

### 3. Use the verification toggle

Roughly 85% of US creators are verified by OnlyFans, but unverified accounts are far more likely to be fake or impersonating other people. Always toggle "Verified Only" unless you're specifically looking for new accounts.

### 4. Use price filters strategically

Free isn't always free — most "Free" creators monetize through PPV and tips. If you want fixed costs, **pick a creator in the $6–$10 range** with active posting (verified by their post count) and turn off PPV in your OnlyFans settings.
